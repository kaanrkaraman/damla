FFmpeg (LGPL v2.1 or later)   https://ffmpeg.org/

FFmpeg is built from the unmodified upstream source with:
  --disable-gpl --disable-nonfree --disable-autodetect --disable-shared --enable-static
  --enable-videotoolbox --enable-audiotoolbox
Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-@FFMPEG_VERSION@.tar.xz
The exact build script is packaging/build-ffmpeg.sh in the Damla repository.
The LGPL text is in ffmpeg.LICENSE next to this file.
